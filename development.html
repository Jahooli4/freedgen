FreedGen App - Live Construction Intelligence


FreedGen is an advanced, AI-driven quality and monitoring platform designed for solar farm construction. Built from real-world, gigawatt-scale project experience, it brings quality control, progress tracking, and reporting into one intelligent system.

Using GPS-tagged photos, drone imagery, and real-time data capture, 3Gen creates a live digital twin of your site, giving clients a real-time view of construction as it happens. Every pile, structure, and module is tracked, verified, and recorded as the project progresses.

The platform highlights issues instantly, from alignment and pile height to torque and installation errors, allowing teams to resolve problems before they escalate into costly delays.

For clients, developers, and investors, 3Gen delivers full transparency. No waiting for reports, no guesswork—just a clear, live view of site progress, quality, and performance at every stage. 3Gen turns your construction site into a fully visible, data-driven operation - in real time.
